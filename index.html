02 About

Intelligence that understands.
And acts.

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Electronic and Information Engineering, Beihang University, advised by Qi Zhao. I also conduct research with the Broadband Communications Research Department at Peng Cheng Laboratory. My expected graduation is May 2027.

My research focuses on computer vision and pattern recognition, embodied AI, multi-agent human-machine collaboration, and vision-language-action models. My experience includes vision-language model training, edge deployment on Ascend 310B, and building embodied human-machine interaction test environments.

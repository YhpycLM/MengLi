About

I am a researcher at Beihang University, working at the intersection of computer vision and robotic science. My work spans vision-language models for robotic manipulation, open-vocabulary grasping, 3D visual grounding, semantic segmentation, and remote-sensing image analysis.

I am particularly interested in building embodied perception systems that connect language, 3D geometry and robotic action — from unified visual-linguistic frameworks for grasping to lightweight 3D perception for vision-language-action (VLA) models.
